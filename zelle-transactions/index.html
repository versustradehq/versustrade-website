VersusTrade accepts enquiries about Zelle transactions. Send your request to the team so they can review it and confirm whether the transaction can be handled.

Zelle requests are subject to confirmation

Zelle-related transactions are considered individually. The team needs to review your request and confirm availability before any transaction is agreed.

  • Explain the transaction you want to make and the amount involved.
  • Wait for the team to confirm whether the request can be processed.
  • Confirm the rate, any applicable charges and the exact instructions before proceeding.

Never share your banking password, Zelle login credentials or one-time verification codes with anyone.

A Zelle enquiry does not mean the transaction is automatically available or approved. Wait for direct confirmation from VersusTrade.
