Before you exchange a gift card

Gift card acceptance and rates can depend on the brand, country or region, currency, card value and whether the card is physical or digital.

  • Share the exact gift card brand and region.
  • Confirm the card format, denomination and current rate.
  • Wait for the team to confirm acceptance and provide instructions before submitting card details.

Not every card or region is necessarily available at all times. Contact the team to confirm your specific card first.

Never send a gift card code or reveal its PIN until the team has confirmed the transaction instructions through an official VersusTrade support channel.
