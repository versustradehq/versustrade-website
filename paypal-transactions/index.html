Need help with a PayPal transaction?

You can send VersusTrade a request involving PayPal. Our team will review the details and confirm whether the requested transaction can be handled before you proceed.

PayPal requests are reviewed individually

PayPal transaction requests are handled on a request-and-confirmation basis. Availability is not guaranteed, and the team may need to review your specific request before confirming whether it can be processed.

  • Briefly describe the PayPal transaction you want to make.
  • Include the relevant amount and currency, without sending passwords or one-time security codes.
  • Wait for the team to confirm availability, the applicable rate, fees if any, and the next steps.

Do not send money or account credentials until you have received and verified the transaction instructions.

A PayPal request is not a confirmed transaction. VersusTrade must first review and confirm whether it can be handled.
